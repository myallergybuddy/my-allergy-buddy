Features

Everything you need to shop and eat with confidence

📊

Barcode Scanning

Scan product barcodes to look up ingredient and allergen information from product databases. Get quick yes/no alerts based on your personal allergy profile.

🔍

Label Photo Scanning

Can't find a barcode? Photograph the ingredient list. On-device text recognition reads the label and flags allergens and "may contain" warnings.

👤

Personal Allergy Profile

Configure your allergens, severity levels, and notes. Every scan is checked against your unique profile—not a generic list.

📋

Scan History

Review past scans so you can quickly check products you've looked up before—handy for repeat grocery trips.

🆘

Emergency Contacts

Save trusted contacts and reach them fast. Share your location via SMS with a maps link when you initiate an emergency alert.

📞

Emergency Dialling

Quick shortcut to dial 000 (Australia) after you confirm—because every second matters in a genuine emergency.

🔒

Passcode Protection

Optionally protect the app with a passcode stored securely on your device, keeping sensitive health information private.

🔔

Push Notifications

Stay informed with app updates and reminders. Control notifications in app and device settings.

Free vs Premium

Free tier includes core allergen detection and scanning—enough to get started and stay safe on everyday shopping trips.

Premium unlocks advanced allergen categories and enhanced features via subscription:

  • Weekly: $3.99 AUD
  • Monthly: $8.99 AUD
  • Yearly: $74.99 AUD

Subscriptions are managed through Google Play or the Apple App Store. See Terms & Conditions for billing details.
