About My Allergy Buddy

Peace of mind at the supermarket, at home, and on the go

Our Mission

Living with food allergies means constant vigilance. My Allergy Buddy was created to make that burden lighter—giving you fast, clear allergen information when you need it most, right from your phone.

Whether you manage your own allergies or care for a child with severe reactions, the app is designed to be simple, fast, and trustworthy.

Built for Australia

We understand Australian food labelling standards and the realities of shopping locally. The app supports Australian emergency dialling (000), uses product data from trusted databases where available, and is tailored to the allergens most relevant to Australian consumers.

How It Works

  • Set up your allergy profile with the allergens and severity levels that matter to you
  • Scan a product barcode or photograph an ingredient label
  • Receive clear alerts when potential allergens are detected
  • Keep a history of scans and store emergency contacts for peace of mind

Important Disclaimer

My Allergy Buddy is an informational tool, not a medical device. It does not replace reading physical packaging, consulting a healthcare professional, or using your own judgment. Always verify ingredients yourself before consuming any product.
